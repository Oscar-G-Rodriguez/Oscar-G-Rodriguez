# Oscar Rodriguez

**Computer science student at the University of Florida building data tools, applied ML projects, and research software.**

I like making a system's inputs, decisions, and limitations visible. My projects range from reproducible backtesting to source-backed public-data analysis and small tools that solve everyday problems.

## Selected work

| Project | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [ROMULUS](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/ROMULUS) | An event-driven **research backtester** with independent strategy portfolios, prior-only champion selection, and decision-level audit files. [Validation and limitations](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/ROMULUS/blob/main/VALIDATION_REPORT.md). |
| [Florida Policy Advisor](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/florida_policy_advisor) | A local **educational prototype** that connects Florida policy indicators to their sources, checks data quality, and evaluates simple forecasting baselines. [Evidence and checks](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/florida_policy_advisor/blob/main/portfolio_evidence/README.md). |
| [Web GPT Lag Remedy](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/WEB-GPT-LAG-REMEDY) | A browser userscript that keeps long ChatGPT conversations responsive by limiting how many messages remain mounted on the page. |
