## Projects

### [SeekR](https://github.com/Hemanka/Shellhacks-X)

SeekR is the project I feel best represents my interests right now. I worked on it with a team to help blind users find objects using a phone camera and voice guidance. We chose vision models to make the project more accessible than a sensor-based setup. During testing, I used SeekR to navigate classrooms and living rooms, move around obstacles, and reach the objects I was looking for.

### [ROMULUS](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/ROMULUS)

ROMULUS is a local financial research app I built to compare trading strategies over time. Each strategy runs on the same historical data in its own simulated portfolio. ROMULUS then considers returns, risk-adjusted performance, and market conditions when choosing a strategy for a separate portfolio to follow. It also evaluates ML forecasts for returns and volatility, so I can inspect the predictions alongside the strategy results. The [validation report](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/ROMULUS/blob/main/VALIDATION_REPORT.md) shows how I checked the strategy and model results.

### [Florida Policy Advisor](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/florida_policy_advisor)

Florida Policy Advisor is a local app I built to explore Florida labor, housing, and fiscal indicators. It checks incoming data for missing values, duplicates, and coverage, then links its results back to the source and retrieval date. It also compares forecasting baselines using time-ordered historical data. I wanted someone using the app to be able to follow a result back to the evidence behind it. The project's [evidence and checks](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/florida_policy_advisor/blob/main/portfolio_evidence/README.md) are documented in its repository.
