## Projects

### [SeekR](https://github.com/Hemanka/Shellhacks-X)

SeekR is the project I feel best represents my interests right now. I worked on it with a team as a prototype for helping blind users find objects using a phone camera and voice guidance. The system still has to judge nearby obstacles from two-dimensional images, and it needs physical testing.

### [ROMULUS](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/ROMULUS)

ROMULUS is a local financial research app I built to compare trading strategies over time. Each strategy runs on the same historical data in its own simulated portfolio. ROMULUS then considers returns, risk-adjusted performance, and market conditions when choosing a strategy for a separate portfolio to follow. It also evaluates ML forecasts for returns and volatility, so I can inspect the predictions alongside the strategy results. A backtest can explain how a strategy behaved on historical data, but it cannot establish how that strategy will perform in live trading. The [validation report](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/ROMULUS/blob/main/VALIDATION_REPORT.md) goes into the checks and limits in more detail.

### [Florida Policy Advisor](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/florida_policy_advisor)

Florida Policy Advisor is a local app I built to explore Florida labor, housing, and fiscal indicators. It checks incoming data for missing values, duplicates, and coverage, then links its results back to the source and retrieval date. When there is enough data, it compares simple forecasting baselines using past observations held out in time. I wanted someone using the app to be able to see what supports a result and where the data is limited. The forecasts help explore trends; they do not show what a policy would cause. The project's [evidence and checks](https://github.com/Oscar-G-Rodriguez/florida_policy_advisor/blob/main/portfolio_evidence/README.md) are documented in its repository.
